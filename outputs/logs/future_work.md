# Future work linked to current limitations

1. Sliding-window or chunk-aggregation classification to address token-budget failures.
2. QLoRA or related low-memory PEFT variants to widen the model search space.
3. Direct encoder-vs-decoder comparison under the same protocol.
4. Post-hoc calibration such as temperature scaling if confidence remains miscalibrated.
5. Larger manually annotated failure taxonomy for mixed sentiment, sarcasm, and late reversals.
6. Finer-grained aspect-level sentiment or rationale extraction for long-form review structure.
